Roadmap to the platinum, in full

Play the story first, then the strangers, then activities, collectibles and crime — and finish with the 100% checklist. Only one trophy can be missed: do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends.

⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia mentions meditating in the kitchen at Jason's house one morning and walks out to the balcony. Follow her, accept, and finish the meditation before Chapter 1 ends: it disappears after that, and it is not confirmed whether the trophy needs it. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of the 18 spots, so this warning is only about the trophy. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story.
